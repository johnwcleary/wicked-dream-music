# Design notes — reference sites

Captured with Playwright on 2 Nov 2026 (full-page screenshots at 1440×900 and 390×844, computed styles pulled from the DOM).

## carrierescene.ca

**What's distinctive.** The whole page is a poster. It runs on three typefaces and almost no colour: near-black `#000002` and a flat light grey `#E4E4E4` do all the work, and a single hot magenta (`#FF60DD`, also the `theme-color`) appears on just three surfaces — the marquee band, the testimonial panel and one tab of the sticky card stack. Display type is a condensed, distressed grotesque ("Early Edition JNL") set uppercase at 140–170px with leading under 1 (126px on 140px) and negative tracking (−2.5%); headings in the "Harber" condensed face sit at 28px/1.0 uppercase for labels and 82px for the H1. Body copy is a small rounded sans (Dosis 16/19). Layout is a strict 12-column grid with 1rem gutters, panels have generous radius (~16px), and the services list is a run of huge one-line rows separated by hairlines, each with a tiny index number (`012`, `015`) and pill tags on the right. It feels confident because nothing is mid-sized: type is either enormous or tiny.

**What to borrow.** For `design/signal`: the size polarity (enormous condensed uppercase vs. small labels, nothing in between); one loud accent used sparingly against two neutrals — our version is the lavender→pink gradient instead of magenta; the services-as-giant-rows pattern with small index numbers and hairline rules; a marquee band for the "Artists. Ideas. Sound. Culture." line; and tight sub-1 leading with negative tracking on display type. Leave behind the distressed/warped type and xerox halftones — they read as grassroots/DIY, which is wrong for a company with major-label credits; our version should be cleaner and more precise.

## meysammotazedi.com

**What's distinctive.** A Framer-built single scroll that reads as one body of work rather than a menu of pages. It opens on a scattered constellation of small black-and-white stills with monospace file-name captions (`screenshot_926901`, `[48.19]`), a "Ready to scroll ↓" cue on the left and a "100%" load counter on the right, with a small "MM©" monogram at centre. After that it alternates full-bleed dithered/halftoned imagery with large passages of tight-leaded grotesque (Neue Haas Grotesk Display, ~90px/0.86, −2% tracking) and narrow editorial columns with tiny metadata lines ("Madrid, 2025, [non-fiction]"). The palette is strictly greyscale apart from one electric-blue highlight on a single link. Motion is mostly scroll-linked reveals and blur. It is 16,000px tall at desktop width, slow on purpose, and the restraint is what makes it feel archival.

**What to borrow.** For `design/archive`: the archival register — monospace captions and index numbers on images, numbered entries, small metadata lines, a single precious accent (our gradient, used on the logo and one hover moment); a tight, large grotesque for statements paired with a mono for labels; image fields of varied aspect ratios; the load counter as a once-per-session intro. Leave behind the heavy dither textures and 16k-pixel scroll length — this client needs the five pages to stay scannable — and the low-contrast grey-on-grey text, which fails AA. Some of its atmosphere (blurred depth, slow drift) also informs `design/dream`.
